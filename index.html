Everything You Need To Know

FAQs

Common questions about dry fruits health, wellness, and wholesome daily nutrition.

Nutritionists recommend a fistful or about 30–40 grams of mixed dry fruits and nuts daily. A balanced daily portion includes 4–5 almonds, 3–4 whole cashews, 10–12 pistachios, 1–2 walnut kernels, and a small pinch of raisins. This delivers an optimal balance of healthy unsaturated fats, plant protein, dietary fiber, and essential minerals without excess caloric intake.
California Almonds and In-Shell Pistachios are the most effective dry fruits for weight management. Rich in plant protein, dietary fiber, and healthy mono-unsaturated fats, they slow down gastric emptying and trigger satiety hormones to keep you fuller for longer. Pistachios are also low in calories per serving and de-shelling them encourages mindful, portion-controlled snacking.
Almonds and Whole Cashews are outstanding for clear, glowing skin. Almonds are nature's richest source of alpha-tocopherol (Vitamin E), an antioxidant that shields skin cells from UV and pollution-induced oxidative damage. Whole cashews supply vital copper and zinc, which actively promote natural collagen synthesis and maintain skin elasticity.
Walnuts and California Almonds provide essential fuel for strong, lustrous hair. Walnuts are packed with plant-based Omega-3 fatty acids (ALA) and biotin, which nourish hair follicles and prevent dry scalp. Almonds supply high iron, magnesium, and Vitamin E to strengthen hair roots and reduce breakage.
For clean and healthy weight gain (lean muscle rather than fat), choose Whole Cashews (Kaju), Sun-Dried Afghani Raisins (Kismis), and Dates (Khajoor). They offer dense, unrefined energy, natural fruit sugars (fructose and glucose), and healthy calories. Pairing a handful of cashews and raisins with a post-workout banana shake or warm milk delivers nutrient-dense mass building.
